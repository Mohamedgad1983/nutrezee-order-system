# 16 — A52: sticker stock is 150 × 100 mm

Date: 2026-09-04 · Owner: "stickers dimension is 15 CM * 10CM printing".

## Change (extension v0.3.9, Console `0.7.48-a48.4`)

The approved label stays designed at 100 × 70 mm (every internal size is in mm). The print sheet becomes
`@page 150mm 100mm`; the cloned label (single and batch) is printed with `zoom: 1.4286` (= 100/70), i.e.
142.86 × 100 mm, filling the sheet height with 3.57 mm side margins (a 2.5 mm pre-zoom left margin).
No reflow and no distortion: the sticker is the preview scaled. The on-screen preview is unchanged.

Why zoom rather than a 150 × 100 redesign: the 100:70 and 150:100 aspect ratios differ (1.43 vs 1.50), a
non-uniform stretch would distort the barcode and text, and the owner approved the current layout.
