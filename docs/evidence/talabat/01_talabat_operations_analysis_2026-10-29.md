## English summary
Volume is stable (KWD 112k / 21.9k orders, −2%), but delivered quality is eroding the brand: 41% of all reviews are 1-star, **≥379 reviews are unanswered**, order delay rate is 9.15% and rising, and the network was offline 122 hours in 30 days, 88% of it "kitchen too busy / too many delayed orders" during the 19:00–22:00 peak. 57% of complaints are missing/wrong items (packing accuracy, not cooking). Cold food is the top review theme in 5 of 8 stores, consistent with orders marked "ready" before they are packed. Promotions ROI is healthy (17% cost, CAC ≈ 3.6 KWD, 83% returning), while paid sponsored ads are regulator-paused, which makes free tStar "Exceptional" visibility the only growth channel; it is locked in 7 of 8 stores, usually by a single goal (complaints <0.5% or rating). Priorities: (1) ready-only-when-sealed + daily wait-time watch, (2) packing checklist incl. customer notes, (3) answer every review within 7 days, (4) immediate food-safety check at Mahboula/Jahra/Jabriya, (5) peak-hour capacity plan and "busy mode" instead of going offline, (6) menu/stock sync, (7) portion specs and heat-retaining packaging, (8) push 5 stores to Exceptional, (9) rescue Ardhiya rating to >4.0, (10) investigate Khairan's −16% demand drop, (11) replicate the burger+potato bundle (+221%) and revisit Rosemary Steak pricing; don't take the portal's 20–50% discount nudges.
