# Project Plan: AI Customer Service System for Nutreeze
## Executive Overview

---

## The Idea in One Sentence

Build an intelligent, **omnichannel** customer service system for Nutreeze, **fully self-hosted** on Milagro VPS, that unifies every customer channel (WhatsApp, website, email, social) into one place and responds with natural Arabic AI grounded in your real data — with zero licensing fees and full data ownership.

---

## Core Components (The Three Layers)

```
Chatwoot (unified intake, MIT license)
        +
Dify (the AI brain — RAG + workflows)
        +
Claude API (natural Arabic responses)
        =
A complete system on Milagro VPS, zero licensing fees
```

---

## Why This Is the Right Project for You (Decision Summary)

| Your Priority | How the Project Delivers It |
|---|---|
| **Lower cost + independence** | Zero licensing fees; everything self-hosted on your existing infrastructure |
| **Omnichannel** | Chatwoot unifies all channels into one inbox |
| **Arabic advantage** | Claude API with natural Gulf Arabic — the blue ocean |
| **Integration with your systems** | Direct connection to ERPNext, BigQuery, and Fleetbase |

**Additional strategic advantage:** the project **absorbs** your current Hermes (WhatsApp) work instead of discarding it — it integrates as one channel inside a larger, unified system.
